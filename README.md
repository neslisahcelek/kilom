A minimal, distraction-free weight tracker for iOS and Android. Step on your scale, snap a photo or type your weight, and get on with your day.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📸 **Scale Photo OCR:** Snap your scale's display or pick from gallery—digits are read automatically using on-device vision (Apple Vision Framework on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android).
- ⚖️ **Instant Context:** See weight difference vs. your last weigh-in (`-0.7 kg` / `+0.4 kg`) and days elapsed at a glance.
- 🔒 **100% On-Device Privacy:** Zero accounts, no tracking, no ads. All photos and data stay on your device and never touch a server.
- 🔄 **Unit Toggle:** Seamlessly switch between `kg` and `lb` anytime.
- 🗑️ **Simple Management:** Swipe left to remove past entries.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) & Compose Multiplatform
- **OCR:** Native Apple Vision (iOS) / Google ML Kit (Android)
- **Persistence:** Multiplatform Settings (Encrypted local storage)
- **Design:** Liquid-glass minimalist interface with native haptics
